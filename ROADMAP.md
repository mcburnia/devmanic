# Devmanic Product Roadmap

## Prioritisation model

Products are assessed against:

- speed to a credible MVP;
- evidence of unmet or poorly served demand;
- search and sharing potential;
- repeat-use potential;
- reuse of existing Devmanic capabilities;
- relevance to software developers;
- differentiation from generic utility sites;
- natural, non-spammy relevance to CRANIS2;
- ongoing maintenance, privacy and regulatory risk.

Estimates are directional and assume AI-assisted development. External approvals are excluded.

## Recommended sequence

### Phase 1 — distribution foundation

1. Complete TracMap rebrand and packaging.
2. Build Devmanic Card.
3. Extract tracked-link capability.
4. Add UTM campaign composition.

### Phase 2 — first signature developer tools

1. SBOM Validator & Viewer.
2. Constrained C/C++ SBOM Generator.
3. Constrained RepoDocs MVP.
4. Delphi SBOM Generator.

### Phase 3 — broaden reach and specialist coverage

1. Image Metadata Inspector.
2. Shift Pay Calculator.
3. Fortran SBOM Generator.
4. Assembly/Firmware SBOM Generator.
5. Hours-to-Goal Tracker.

### Phase 4 — deepen the ecosystem

1. Repo-to-TracMap.
2. SBOM Comparator.
3. Legacy-Code SBOM Workbench.
4. Licence and security tools.
5. Remaining general-purpose microapps.

## Delivery rule

A strategic product enters active development only with a sharply constrained MVP. “Universal repository understanding” and “complete SBOM discovery” are visions, not acceptable first-release scopes.
