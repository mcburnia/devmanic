# Devmanic

**Useful software tools that do one thing properly.**

Devmanic is a value-first collection of free and affordable microapplications for everyday users and software developers. Its tools should be useful in their own right: immediate, understandable, privacy-conscious and free from unnecessary subscriptions or intrusive advertising.

Devmanic also provides a natural, restrained way for software developers and manufacturers to discover [CRANIS2](https://cranis2.com), without turning free tools into adverts.

## Product principles

Every Devmanic product should aim to be:

- useful before promotional;
- usable without registration wherever persistence is unnecessary;
- mobile-first, accessible and multilingual-ready;
- transparent about calculations, assumptions and limitations;
- privacy-preserving and local-first where files or source code are involved;
- exportable, deletable and free from data lock-in;
- independently shareable through a permanent, indexable landing page;
- subtle and contextually relevant when mentioning CRANIS2.

## Roadmap

The roadmap deliberately contains two delivery tracks:

1. **Quick-to-market microapps** — generally hours or a few days to build.
2. **Signature developer tools** — more differentiated products delivered through tightly constrained MVPs.

## Current products

### Tracked QR Generator

The first live Devmanic application generates QR codes and records aggregate scan-through activity.

- Live service: [qr.devmanic.com](https://qr.devmanic.com)
- Status: live
- Strategic role: broad acquisition, repeat analytics visits and reusable tracking infrastructure

### Devmanic TracMap

A reusable interactive schematic mapping component for presenting product journeys, processes and system flows. Lines show journeys; stations show important stages; selecting a station reveals contextual information.

- Status: existing component being rebranded and packaged
- Tagline: **Map the journey. Explore the detail.**

## Relationship with CRANIS2

Devmanic earns attention by providing value first. CRANIS2 may be referenced where it is genuinely relevant—for example, where a free SBOM tool solves a point-in-time task and CRANIS2 provides continuous lifecycle management.

Devmanic tools must not:

- interrupt users with unrelated CRANIS2 advertising;
- make misleading security, compliance, legal or tax claims;
- create doorway pages whose main purpose is manipulating search rankings;
- sell arbitrary backlinks or invite link spam;
- imply that a free point-in-time check proves continuous compliance.
