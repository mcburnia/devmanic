## Other retained concepts

### Repo-to-TracMap

Use the RepoDocs evidence graph to propose product, data, security, deployment and incident journeys. Human review determines primary paths, branches, business meaning and regulatory context.

### SBOM Validator & Viewer

Validate CycloneDX and SPDX, explain errors, present components accessibly and provide an editable hand-off into the legacy-code workbench.

### SBOM Comparator

Compare releases, including added/removed/changed components, licence changes and unresolved identifiers.

### Licence Compatibility Checker

Provide technical guidance on likely compatibility, attribution and copyleft obligations. It must state that it is not legal advice.

### Developer security utilities

- website security/privacy checker;
- security headers generator;
- guided CVSS calculator;
- CRA product classification assistant;
- CRA technical-documentation readiness checker.

## Backlog governance

Each item should record:

- user problem and evidence;
- target users;
- smallest credible MVP;
- excluded scope;
- shared capabilities;
- privacy/security risks;
- CRANIS2 relationship;
- validation measures;
- promotion criteria into the active roadmap.

An item is promoted only when its constrained MVP and success measure are explicit.
