# Quick-Build Product Queue

## Definition

A quick win should normally:

- deliver a credible MVP within two development days;
- avoid external approval or proprietary data dependencies;
- have a clear single-purpose landing page;
- work without an account unless persistence is intrinsic;
- produce a useful outcome in under a minute;
- require limited ongoing maintenance.

Devmanic Card is retained near the top despite exceeding two days because it reuses the QR platform and has unusually strong distribution potential.

## Queue

### 1. Devmanic TracMap packaging

**Outcome:** Rebrand and package the existing dynamic component as Devmanic TracMap.

**MVP**

- consistent TracMap naming;
- existing line, station, branch and feeder rendering;
- dynamic station context panel;
- accessible keyboard interaction;
- reusable data configuration;
- README and working example;
- versioned rollback point before rebrand.

**Do not add:** repository analysis or documentation generation.

### 2. Devmanic Card

**Outcome:** A digital business card stored in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

**MVP**

- card editor and mobile public page;
- vCard download;
- stable tracked QR;
- Apple generic pass;
- Google Wallet Generic Pass;
- scan, page-view and contact-save counts;
- field-level visibility and unpublish/delete controls;
- relevant “Created with Devmanic” return link.

Apple certificates and Google issuer approval are external dependencies.

### 3. Tracked Short Links

**Outcome:** Editable short links with privacy-preserving click analytics.

**MVP**

- generated and custom aliases;
- editable destination;
- expiry and optional click limit;
- aggregate referrer, country and device categories;
- QR generation;
- malware/phishing controls and abuse reporting.

### 4. UTM Campaign Builder

**Outcome:** Consistent campaign links connected to existing tracking.

**MVP**

- source, medium, campaign, term and content;
- normalisation and naming conventions;
- saved campaign collection;
- generated short link and QR;
- CSV import/export.

### 5. Image Metadata Inspector & Cleaner

**Outcome:** Inspect and remove sensitive image metadata locally.

**MVP**

- EXIF/IPTC/XMP inspection;
- clear GPS, device and timestamp warnings;
- remove location only or all nonessential metadata;
- local browser processing;
- before/after comparison.

### 6. Shift Pay Calculator

**Outcome:** Calculate pay across real-world shift-rate bands.

**MVP**

- start/end and unpaid breaks;
- base, evening, night, weekend and holiday rates;
- overtime threshold/multiplier;
- gross-pay breakdown and effective hourly rate;
- printable/shareable result.

### 7. Hours-to-Goal Tracker

**Outcome:** Accumulate sessions towards a lifetime target.

**MVP**

- timer and manual sessions;
- cumulative target;
- progress and projected completion;
- local storage;
- JSON/CSV import/export.

### 8. Subscription Reality Calculator

**Outcome:** Expose the real cost of recurring subscriptions.

**MVP**

- introductory and standard price;
- billing frequency and increases;
- one-, five- and ten-year totals;
- effective monthly cost;
- cancellation reminder download.

### 9. Deadline Chain Calculator

**Outcome:** Calculate a safe project start date from a final deadline.

**MVP**

- working days and excluded dates;
- dependent tasks;
- contingency;
- latest safe start;
- printable schedule and calendar export.

### 10. Done List

**Outcome:** Track completed work rather than unfinished tasks.

**MVP**

- rapid accomplishment entry;
- category and optional duration;
- daily/weekly summaries;
- status/appraisal-ready export;
- local-first storage.

## Common release checklist

- dedicated permanent URL;
- indexable explanatory content and examples;
- accessible mobile and desktop behaviour;
- privacy statement appropriate to the data used;
- transparent formulas/assumptions;
- export/delete controls where data persists;
- modest contextual Devmanic attribution;
- no forced CRANIS2 promotion.
